PlanTrip v1: from a board of cards to decisions

Capture fast, keep one entry per real place, compare in a matrix, and never forget why something was dropped.

SymptomNear-duplicate cards, compare page rarely used, “why did we drop that?” forgotten.
CauseThe unit is a free-form card. No place identity, no shared criteria, no drop reason.
FixInbox → pinned places → two-level decisions → matrix with “to check” cells → drop requires a reason.
OutcomeThe group sees one ranked grid per decision; dropped options keep their reason; the Butler fills gaps, humans decide.

Example data is illustrative, loosely based on trip 3 (Martinique villa bundle vs Algarve vs Açores vs a Rep Dom all-inclusive package). Prices are placeholders, not quotes.

Same place? “Majestic Mirage” is 40 m from an existing place.
  • Search uses OSM place search (Nominatim). Tap a result to save its coordinates and OSM id.
  • Fallback: paste a map link that contains coordinates, or drop the pin by hand. Airbnb villas often need this.
  • Same place? The app asks when two places share an OSM id or are within about 150 m. Merging keeps the other name as an alias.
  • Unpinned places show 📍 to pin. The Butler can suggest a pin; a person confirms.

Tradeoff: the 150 m check could flag two neighbouring resorts. That's why it asks instead of auto-merging.

  • Top level: destinations or packages compete against each other.
  • Inside a destination: sub-decisions compare its parts (villa against villa, flight against flight).
  • A package is a single option with a single price, so it competes directly with a bundle.

Not in v1: automatically summing a bundle's parts into the destination total (that's v1.2, with costs per origin group). For now, someone fills the “cost per person” cell by hand.

← swipe for more options →

  • The first column stays fixed and options swipe sideways, one column per snap. The page itself never scrolls sideways.
  • Cells hold a value and an optional src link. Empty cells say to check.
  • Ranking is cost per person, then 👍 count. No hidden weights.
  • Dropped options move to the end, greyed out, and show their reason.
  • Tap an option's name to open its photos, sources and comments.

Note: in this example the Rep Dom package costs less but ranks 3rd. Strictly following “cost first” would put it 1st. Open question: should 👍 count first, or cost first? The mockup shows the votes-first result so you can see how it feels.

8 · What the Butler can and can't do it fills cells, humans decide

Butler canButler cannotWhy
Fill to check cells (value + source)Create options or decisionsCreating cards directly caused most of the duplicates
Add finds to the inboxMerge placesMerges are hard to undo
Suggest pins, merges, dropsDrop or reviveThe group owns its decisions
Answer questions as commentsChange who is chosenOnly the organizer records the final choice

Each run reports something like “Filled 9 of 14 cells · 3 inbox items · 2 suggestions”, so its work can be measured. Your local Hermes uses an owner token that doesn't need a run id. Runs queued from the site keep the run-id lock.
